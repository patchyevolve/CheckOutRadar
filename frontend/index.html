Regulatory & Consumer Context

The Hidden Cost of Deceptive Checkout Funnels

On November 30, 2023, India's Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) notified the Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns. Despite regulatory mandates, fee concealment remains pervasive.

💸

Late-Stage Fee Inflation (Drip Pricing)

Platform fees, handling charges, surge pricing, and convenience levies routinely appear only on the final checkout screen, after consumers have already invested time entering address and delivery credentials.

🛒

Pre-Ticked Add-ons (Basket Sneaking)

Optional services like transit guarantees, extended warranties, and tip donations are selected by default at cart, forcing consumers to affirmatively uncheck boxes to avoid unintended charges.

⏳

Artificial Urgency & Time Pressure

Deceptive countdown timers claim prices or inventory are expiring within minutes, yet reset automatically upon page refresh or across sessions without genuine inventory depletion.

The Five-Stage Outside-In Auditing Workflow

Checkout Radar audits merchant checkouts strictly from the outside in, operating identically to an autonomous consumer browser session across controlled permutations.

STAGE 01

Target & Merchant Discovery

TinyFish Search API

Discovers official merchant checkout funnels, canonical product URLs, and policy documentation endpoints automatically, avoiding hardcoded navigation paths.

Input: Merchant Query / Domain Output: Verified Funnel & Policy URLs Audit Log: provider_calls (operation: search)
STAGE 02

Policy & Fee Threshold Extraction

TinyFish Fetch API + TTL Cache

Crawls terms of service, shipping policies, and refund rules. Caches policy clauses with TTL to distinguish legitimate small-cart thresholds from hidden fees without redundant network calls.

Input: Policy URLs Output: Free Shipping Thresholds & Flat Fee Clauses Safeguard: ETag / Cache-Control validation
STAGE 03

Multi-Condition Web Agent Navigation

TinyFish Lite Web Agent

Launches durable, lease-fenced browser agent runs across an expanded condition matrix: Tier-1 vs Tier-2 Pincodes, Fresh vs Returning Profiles, Above vs Below Cart Thresholds, and Temporal Re-observations.

Input: Cart Recipe Family + Matrix Permutations Captured Stages: Product Page · Cart Drawer · Checkout Summary Durable Worker: Lease generation fencing
STAGE 04

Deterministic Matched-Pair Differential Engine

5-Rule Diff Engine

Compares condition runs that differ in exactly one requested variable while holding all others fixed. Separates cart recipe quantities from unit prices to ensure legitimate quantity changes never trigger false price alarms.

Diff Rules: Price Variance · Late Disclosure · Small Cart Policy · Temporal Drift · Addon Detection Invariance: Strict recipe family separation
STAGE 05

Evidence Pipeline & Cryptographic Chain of Custody

Redactor + SHA-256 Hasher + CCPA Mapper

Applies privacy redaction masks to viewport captures, computes cryptographic SHA-256 digests, normalizes currency to minor units (paise), and formats verifiable HTML reports and NCH grievance petitions.

Custody: Immutable SHA-256 digest on every viewport Outputs: Scorecard · Evidence Gallery · CCPA Grievance Draft · HTML Report

Explicit Scope, Boundaries & Legal Neutrality

To protect audit integrity and prevent unwarranted claims of statutory guilt, Checkout Radar maintains rigorous technical boundaries:

Non-Adjudicative Technical Indicators

Findings serve as technical empirical checkout audit evidence for researchers, journalists, and consumer advocates. They do not constitute formal judicial determinations or statutory adjudication.

Observed Automated Flow Boundary

Audits certify only the observed automated journey under tested parameters. Uninspected third-party payment gateways (UPI, netbanking, card OTP screens) and unselected options (e.g. COD fees) are explicit scope boundaries.

Privacy Redaction Boundary

Form inputs (credit card numbers, CVVs, passwords) render in upper/middle screen areas and require coordinate-based masks. Automated default redaction masks bottom 5% utility bars.
