1. Problem Statement
The Hidden Fee Epidemic & The Evidence Gap
Consumers face systemic late-stage fee inflation without any tool to gather admissible legal proof.
💸 LATE-STAGE INFLATION
The Drip Pricing Trap
  • Surge fees, handling charges, and packaging costs appear only on the final payment screen.
  • Consumers invest 10–15 minutes entering addresses before the true payable price is disclosed.
  • Violates Section 18 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
⚖️ REGULATORY MANDATE
CCPA 2023 Guidelines
  • Notified by Ministry of Consumer Affairs on 30 Nov 2023.
  • Annex 1.8: Strictly bans Drip Pricing (concealing mandatory costs).
  • Annex 1.2: Prohibits Basket Sneaking (pre-ticked add-ons).
  • Annex 1.1: Prohibits False Urgency (falsely resetting countdown timers).
🔍 THE EVIDENCE VOID
Lack of Empirical Tooling
  • Existing compliance tools cater exclusively to internal platform teams.
  • Consumers, researchers, and journalists lack outside-in auditing mechanisms.
  • Modern SPAs (React/Shopify) defeat simple scrapers, leaving deceptive funnels unmonitored.

2. Proposed Solution
Matched-Pair Differential Auditing Engine
Controlled behavioral experiments that mathematically isolate fee inflation and dark patterns.
Not a Scraper: A Controlled Experiment
  • Differential Principle: Compares two checkout runs that differ in exactly one requested dimension while holding all other controls fixed.
  • Integer Minor-Unit Math: Reconciles all charges in integer paise (₹0.01) to eliminate floating-point rounding errors.
  • The Cart Invariant: Separates Recipe Family from Cart Instance. Quantity changes do NOT trigger false price variance alarms.
  • Cryptographic Custody: 1280×720 viewports redacted with Pillow and hashed with SHA-256 for court-admissible proof.
📍 Location Effect
Tests Tier-1 Metro (110001) vs Tier-2/3 (492001) · Controls: Session, Cart, Recipe
👤 Session Effect
Tests Fresh Guest Profile vs Returning Profile · Controls: Pincode, Cart, Recipe
🛒 Cart Threshold
Tests Above Threshold (₹998) vs Below (₹499) · Controls: Pincode, Session, Unit Price
⏱️ Temporal Drift
Tests Observation at t0 vs Re-observation at t1 · Controls: All conditions held fixed
